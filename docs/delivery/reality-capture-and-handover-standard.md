# Reality Capture, Commissioning and Handover Standard

## Field truth
Site observations, inspections and reality captures are evidence about constructed reality. They do not automatically overwrite approved design intent or verified Project Truth.

## Inspection and quality
- Approved inspection test plans define acceptance criteria and hold/witness points.
- Failed inspections create or reference a non-conformance where required.
- Use-as-is and redesign NCR dispositions require explicit authorised deviation approval.
- Critical NCRs block affected release, certification and handover states until resolved.

## Site change control
Every material site change records reason, source, affected requirements, drawings/models, commercial effect, programme effect and criticality. Changes are not implemented as silent drawing edits.

## Reality capture
1. Register capture source, time, coordinate reference and responsible actor.
2. Process image/360/drone/point-cloud/LiDAR evidence against the correct governed model version.
3. Record detected deviations with measured value and applicable tolerance where available.
4. AI may classify and prioritise; it may not autonomously accept major or critical deviations.
5. Human disposition links the deviation to acceptance, NCR or design change.

## Commissioning
Commissioning records reference the approved procedure, measured readings, witnesses, evidence and defects. Failed tests block operational promotion. Conditional tests remain visible until their conditions are formally closed.

## Building and material passports
The handover graph carries asset identity, model object, manufacturer/product information, serial/batch data, warranties, controlled documents, commissioning evidence, maintenance requirements and material circularity data where available.

## Digital twin
Telemetry bindings are configuration-controlled links between an asset passport and an external provider identity/schema. Missing or degraded telemetry does not alter authoritative asset records. Operational analytics must distinguish measured, inferred and unavailable values.

## Offline site mode
Offline captures are signed by device/user identity, timestamped locally, and synchronised with conflict detection. A local mutation never silently overwrites a newer server version. Media integrity is verified after upload.
