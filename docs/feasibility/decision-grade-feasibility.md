# Decision-Grade Feasibility Standard

Concept Spaces must not represent a feasibility scenario as decision-grade merely because the arithmetic is complete.

## Required evidence chain

1. Site truth with source/confidence and verified geometry basis.
2. Applicable regulatory envelope with jurisdiction/version/effective-date provenance.
3. Programme brief linked to client priorities and typology knowledge sources.
4. Climate/environmental context with datasets, assumptions and simulation evidence where quantitative claims are made.
5. Development scenario with explicit built-up/saleable/leasable area bases.
6. Economic assumptions with category, unit, effective date, source and confidence.
7. Sensitivity analysis on material cost, value, duration, absorption and finance assumptions.
8. Decision ledger entry recording why a scenario is selected, rejected or held.

## Critical unknowns

Material D-confidence assumptions keep the scenario non-decision-grade until they are verified, accepted as an explicit risk by authorised decision makers, or the scenario is abandoned.

## Precedent intelligence

Precedent analysis extracts transferable principles and benchmark evidence. It must not copy protected design expression or promote precedent into statutory/project requirements without explicit justification.

## Environmental claims

Quantitative sustainability claims require identified datasets/engines, immutable inputs and reproducible result evidence. Qualitative design guidance must remain labelled qualitative.
