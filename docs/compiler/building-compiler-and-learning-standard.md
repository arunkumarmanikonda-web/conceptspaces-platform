# Building Compiler™, Building Git and Governed Learning Standard

## Compilation model
The Building Compiler is an orchestration and assurance framework, not a single generative model. It compiles a versioned input snapshot through Project Truth, applicable regulation, programme, feasibility, option generation, architecture, engineering, interiors, quantities/cost, coordination and release assurance.

Each stage records:
- immutable input and output hashes;
- deterministic/rule engine references where applicable;
- agent runs and prompt/model versions where AI contributes;
- assumptions and confidence;
- validation findings and evidence;
- criticality and accountable review state.

C3/C4 outputs may be prepared or analysed by AI within approved bounds but cannot self-issue.

## Intent translation
Text, voice, sketch and markup are first converted into a structured Intent Command. Ambiguity is surfaced as questions. Low-confidence or critical commands require human confirmation before execution.

## Pareto exploration
Generative exploration may create many candidate options across yield, environment, architecture, capex and other approved metrics. Constraint-violating options remain visible as failed/conditional evidence and are never promoted as compliant solely because they score well on another objective.

## Building Git
Alternative design states live on explicit project branches. Object-level commits record parent hashes, changed objects, author type and message. A merge is itself a governed change and runs conflict, compliance and impact analysis before altering the approved branch.

## Change Impact Engine™
Every material change can trace downstream requirements, rules, disciplines, documents, model objects, BOQ lines, contracts, cost and schedule. Decision Reversal Cost is an estimate with stated confidence, not a fabricated exact value.

## Design Linter™
Lint rules distinguish statutory rules, deterministic engineering constraints, coordination checks and design heuristics. Their source and authority level remain visible. Unknown statutory input produces Not Verified rather than a guessed pass.

## Adversarial review
AI Design Review Council and Red Team reviews challenge design assumptions independently from the generating agent. Constructability, operability and maintainability reviews are first-class review modes. Unresolved critical findings block acceptance.

## Design Genome™
The platform never learns directly from one project outcome or from its own generated text. Learning candidates move through observation, evidence, privacy review, expert review, benchmark, shadow operation and controlled production. Promotion requires measurable evidence, expert reviewers and a rollback reference.

## Ask Your Project™
Grounded answers cite versioned project resources. Conflicts and missing facts are surfaced. C3/C4 answers are advisory and cannot substitute for professional approval, authority determination or contractual certification.
