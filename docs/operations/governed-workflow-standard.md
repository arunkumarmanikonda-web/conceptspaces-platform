# Governed Workflow Standard

Concept Spaces workflows coordinate work but never obscure authority.

## Version binding

Workflow definitions are versioned. Running instances remain bound to the version with which they started unless an explicit migration is authorised and recorded.

## Maker-checker

Where independent review is configured, the maker and checker must be different accountable principals. Automated routing may support the control but cannot satisfy the checker role merely because a second software agent executed.

## Evidence

Controlled steps may require evidence types before submission or approval. Missing evidence blocks the step rather than being inferred.

## SLA and escalation

Due times, SLA state and escalation are explicit. Escalation changes visibility and responsibility but does not auto-approve overdue critical work.

## Risk and compliance

Risks, controls, obligations, assessments and audit findings retain source, owner, state, evidence and review dates. AI may identify possible risk/compliance conditions but formal acceptance or closure remains governed.

## Analytics

KPI observations retain calculation reference, source and confidence. Dashboard precision must never imply a higher level of evidence than the underlying data supports.
